Bering 88

Our Yacht

Purpose-Built for Polar Exploration

The Bering 88 is a true expedition vessel — compact enough to reach waters inaccessible to cruise ships, yet equipped with the comfort and amenities of a luxury private yacht.

We anchor and stay in bays where larger vessels can only send tenders with organised groups. You enjoy more time ashore, no queues, and the serenity of knowing you set the pace.
