Where the Andes Meet the Sea

The Chilean Patagonia fjords offer a dramatic prelude to Antarctic exploration. Snow-capped peaks plunge into deep blue waters, glaciers calve into the sea, and mist drifts through channels that few vessels ever navigate.

Our boutique yacht reaches anchorages and bays that cruise ships pass by entirely — the same exclusive approach that defines every Polar Meridian voyage.
