Bering 88

Trip Summary

  • Access limited to January and February when pack ice allows
  • Emperor penguins on the Ross Sea ice
  • World's largest Adélie penguin colony at Cape Adare
  • Historic huts of Shackleton and Scott
  • Ross Ice Shelf and towering tabular icebergs
  • Mt Erebus and McMurdo Sound
  • Subantarctic Islands — Snares, Auckland, Campbell, Macquarie
  • 24-hour sunlight in the Ross Sea
  • Orca feeding amongst the ice floes

The Heart of Antarctica

Due to pack ice, access to the Ross Sea is limited to just two months when wildlife thrives. This is expedition cruising at its most authentic — navigating through ice that guards the entrance to one of Earth's last frontiers.

Walk in Shackleton's footsteps, gaze at Mt Erebus, and witness the endless expanse of the Ross Ice Shelf from the deck of your private yacht.
