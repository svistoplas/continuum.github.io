Discover New Lands

We are convinced that not all islands are yet discovered. Unique teammates with diverse backgrounds join each voyage, bringing fresh perspectives to every landing.

Unlike large cruise ships that rush between ports, we anchor and stay. More time ashore, no lines, no crowds — just you, your shipmates, and the vast white continent.

Tentative Route

Flexible itinerary — exact landings depend on weather and ice conditions.

Ushuaia — Departure. 3 days crossing the Drake Passage, then immediate landing.
St George Island — Bellingshausen station and island exploration.
Greenwich Island — Penguin colony visits.
Livingston Island — Shore landings and wildlife observation.
Deception Island — Volcanic caldera, historic whaling station.
Whalers Bay — Hiking at the former whale fat processing base.
Trinity Island — Zodiac landing among ice sculptures.
Port Lockroy — Historic British base and post office.
Lemaire Channel — Dramatic passage between 1 km-tall rock faces.
Enterprise Island — Wreck diving and glacier views.
Antarctic Continent — Mountain landings on the mainland.
Chilean Antarctic Station — Personal area of Chile.
Argentinian Base — Research station visit.
Paradise Cove — Final anchorage in one of Antarctica's most beautiful bays.
