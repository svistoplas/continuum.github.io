For Antarctica: Choose Options

Skip Drake Strait (Fly & Cruise) ▼

Although the Drake Passage is an experience in itself, it is quite rough and might want to be avoided by people who are put off by the thought of constant motion. The seas around the peninsula are generally much calmer and the boat will be far more manageable for people with sea sickness tendencies.

Punta Arenas → King George Island → Ushuaia → Buenos Aires

Fly Punta Arenas to King George Island in partnership with DAP Airlines, then sail to Ushuaia and continue to Buenos Aires by private jet charter. Two nights of stay in Punta Arenas (in case of flight delay due to weather) and one night of stay in Buenos Aires are included.

Buenos Aires → Ushuaia → King George Island → Punta Arenas

Fly Buenos Aires to Ushuaia, sail to King George Island, then return to Punta Arenas by air with DAP Airlines. One night of stay in Buenos Aires and one night in Punta Arenas are included.

Total Fly & Cruise

Punta Arenas ↔ King George Island roundtrip by air. Join the yacht at King George Island and explore the Antarctic Peninsula without crossing the Drake Passage at all.

Vinson Massif Day Trip (4,200 m)

Add an airplane day roundtrip to the base of the highest peak of Antarctica — Vinson Massif (4,200 m), in partnership with Antarctica Logistics & Expeditions (ALE).
